# The State of the Realm — 2026

**Reference file. Not published.** Read this before writing any dispatch, so
you know whether what happened was ordinary or a scandal. A house losing is
not a story. A *great* house losing to a *fallen* one is the whole story.

Facts here are the 2025 season and the 2026 preseason outlook. Update the
standings weekly as the season runs.

---

## Last winter, in one breath

**Seattle won Super Bowl LX, 29–13 over New England**, on 8 February 2026 at
Levi's Stadium — the second title in the house's history. Seattle came through
as the NFC's top seed; New England came out of the AFC as the 2 seed, having
gone through Los Angeles (Chargers), Houston, and finally Denver 10–7 in the
snow.

The two of them opened the 2026 season against each other. Seattle won that
too, 13–10.

## 2026, as it happens

**Week 1** — Seahawks 13, Patriots 10 (Darnold out, hip; Drew Lock relieved
him and won it). **49ers 27, Rams 7** at the Melbourne Cricket Ground, the
first NFL game in Australia — Los Angeles landed about 24 hours before
kickoff and left after 36; San Francisco had been there a week. Stafford was
pulled, uninjured, with 3:05 left. Myles Garrett's Rams debut: no tackles.

Read that one as a Great House losing to an Ascendant one *badly* — not an
upset by the table, but a 20-point beating of the realm's favourite by a
house ranked twelfth. The 49ers are the second-oldest roster in the league
and everyone had used that as a reason to file them away. Revisit the tiers
if this holds.

### What Week 1 says about the tiers

- **Los Angeles (Chargers)** losing at home to the bottom-ranked house in the
  realm is the week's one genuine scandal. Both L.A. houses lost; the Rams were
  beaten by 20 in Melbourne on the Thursday.
- **Baltimore** (ranked third on an 8-9 season) and **Jacksonville** both looked
  the part immediately. **Cincinnati**'s defensive spending paid on day one.
- **Arizona, the Jets and Las Vegas** — three of the five Fallen houses — all
  won. If any of that holds past September, the Fallen tier needs rewriting, and
  the upset table with it.
- **Carolina** scored 37 and lost by 22. A Diminished house is allowed to be
  outclassed; being outclassed while playing well is a different diagnosis.
- **Seven** new stewards ran their first regular-season game in charge:
  LaFleur (Arizona), Minter (Baltimore), John Harbaugh (New York Giants),
  Saleh (Tennessee), McCarthy (Pittsburgh), Monken (Cleveland) and
  Hafley (Miami). Week 1 2026 is, more than anything, about new management.
  Four of the seven won: LaFleur, Minter, Harbaugh and McCarthy. Saleh, Monken
  and Hafley lost.

## The hierarchy going into 2026

Ordered by preseason power ranking. Tiers are what the *realm believes* in
September — the whole point of the file is that reality will disagree.

### The Great Houses
Expected to contend. Losing to anyone below them is a story.

**Rams** (12-5, lost NFC title) — the betting favorite, above the champion ·
**Seahawks** (14-3, champions) · **Ravens** (8-9!) · **Eagles** (11-6) ·
**Patriots** (14-3, lost SB) · **Bills** (12-5)

### The Ascendant
Real teams with a real claim. A run from any of these surprises no one.

**Broncos** (14-3, 1 seed, lost AFC title at home) · **Chiefs** (6-11!) ·
**Lions** (9-8) · **Packers** (9-7-1) · **Bears** (11-6) · **49ers** (12-5)

## Two houses out of position — use these

**House Chiefs went 6-11.** The dynasty that everyone organized their decade
around simply stopped. And yet the realm has them back in the top ten for
2026, because nobody believes it. That gap — between what happened and what
people will accept — is the most loaded story in the league. Play it as a
house in denial, or a house whose fall was exaggerated. Never as a normal team.

**House Ravens went 8-9** and are ranked third for 2026. Same shape, quieter.
A great house that had one bad year and expects the realm to forget it.

Conversely: **Denver went 14-3, earned the top seed, and lost the conference
at home, 10–7.** No one is talking about them. They should be furious.

## What counts as an upset

Cross-tier losses, by severity:

| Loser | Winner | Read it as |
|---|---|---|
| Great House | Fallen | A scandal. Lead with it. Someone's claim is in question. |
| Great House | Diminished | A serious embarrassment; the loser's whole week is about it. |
| Great House | Contested | Ordinary. Not the story. |
| Ascendant | Fallen | Notable — the frame is *pretenders exposed*. |
| Anyone | Anyone same tier | A war, not an intrigue. Write the collision, not the shock. |

**A caution:** tiers are September opinion. By November this file should be
edited to match what's actually happening, and a house that keeps beating its
tier gets promoted. Nothing is more tired than a blog still calling a 9-2 team
plucky underdogs.
