## The Wound

exactly one. This is the engine of the whole house — write it first.

## The Current Lord
The QB. Not stats — temperament. Is he a boy king, a usurper, a steady hand
nobody respects, a mercenary on a one-year contract of convenience?

## The Court

the one nobody trusts.

## Ancient Grudges
