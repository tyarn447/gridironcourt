## The Wound

This house is thirty-two years old and has never won anything. Two trips to
the final field, two defeats — the second, in February 2016, by a team it
had beaten during the season, at the end of a year it had spent looking
unbeatable.

And then last season it won the division at **8–9** and hosted a playoff
game, which is the strangest possible thing to be proud of. They lost it
34–31 to the Rams. The realm treated the whole affair as an administrative
error.

## The Lord

**Bryce Young.** First overall pick, written off almost immediately, and
then — genuinely — better. He has already survived the part of the story
where everyone decides you are a bust, which is more than most survive.

## The Knights

- A quiet spring by design. This house is not finished being built.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Falcons, House Saints, House Buccaneers.** The division. Carolina
  is the youngest and least respected of the four and won it anyway.

## What Changed This Spring

Very little. **Andy Dalton** left for Philadelphia. Carolina won a division,
hosted a playoff game, and then had one of the quietest offseasons in the
realm — a house that either believes in what it has or could not attract
anyone.

## Prophecy

That 8–9 was a beginning and not a fluke. The realm firmly expects a fluke.
