## The Wound

This house has never won. Not once. It has been to the final field three
times and lost all three, the most recent in February 2022 by three points,
with a lord who had been in the league two years and looked like the future
of the sport.

Then the future kept getting hurt. That is the Cincinnati wound — not defeat
exactly, but the specific torture of a house that has the pieces and cannot
keep them upright.

## The Lord

**Joe Burrow.** Cool to the point of insolence, plainly great, and made of
something more fragile than this house can afford. When he plays a full
season they are dangerous. He has not often played a full season.

## The Knights

- **Dexter Lawrence** — acquired from New York, an immovable object for a
  house that has spent years being moved.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Steelers, House Ravens.** Cincinnati is the youngest sibling in the
  most brutal division in the realm and has been treated accordingly for
  fifty years.

## What Changed This Spring

They finally spent on the Watch — **Lawrence** by trade, **Boye Mafe** for
sixty million, **Bryan Cook**, plus an extension for **Orlando Brown** to
keep the lord upright. Six wins last year. This is a house that has decided
the problem was never the offense.

## Prophecy

That the lord stays standing for seventeen games. That's all. That's the
whole prophecy.
