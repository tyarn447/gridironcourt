## The Wound

This house has moved three times. Oakland, Los Angeles, Oakland again, and
now the desert — and each time it took the same swagger to a city that had
not asked for it.

The swagger is the wound. *Just Win, Baby* is the most famous motto in the
sport and this house has won three games in a season. It has not won a
playoff game since January 2003. The identity is entirely intact and
entirely unearned, and everyone can see it.

## The Lord

**Kirk Cousins**, thirty-eight, on twenty million guaranteed — and listed
ahead of **Fernando Mendoza**, the first-round rookie everyone actually came
to see.

That ordering is the whole house in one line: the safe, expensive, temporary
choice over the interesting one. They will get to Mendoza. They always get
there eventually, usually too late.

## The Knights

- **Tyler Linderbaum** — eighty-one million for a centre, which is the most
  serious thing this house has done in years and the least glamorous.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Chiefs.** The long humiliation.
- **House Broncos, House Chargers.** Divisional, and conducted with more
  volume than consequence.

## What Changed This Spring

**Cousins**, **Linderbaum**, **Kwity Paye**, **Nakobe Dean** — and
**Geno Smith** sent to the Jets. A house buying competence in bulk after
three wins, while the rookie waits.

## Prophecy

That Mendoza plays before Christmas, and that everyone pretends this was the plan.
