## The Wound

This house has moved three times. Oakland, Los Angeles, Oakland again, and
now the desert — and each time it took the same swagger to a city that had
not asked for it.

The swagger is the wound. *Just Win, Baby* is the most famous motto in the
sport and this house has won three games in a season. It has not won a
playoff game since January 2003. The identity is entirely intact and
entirely unearned, and everyone can see it.

## The Uncrowned

This house held the **first overall pick** and spent it on **Fernando
Mendoza**. First in the entire realm. The single most valuable thing a losing
house is given, and the only real compensation for a year of humiliation.

He is the backup.

**Kirk Cousins** — thirty-eight, twenty million guaranteed, on his fourth
house — will take the field first, and the young man the whole desert came
to see will hold a clipboard and watch. The steward, **Klint Kubiak**, is in
his first year here and was asked when Mendoza might play. His answer was
*"let's work to Miami first,"* which is what a man says when he does not
have an answer and would prefer you stopped asking.

The cruelty is in the calendar. There is no bye until Week 13. There is no
Thursday game, no Monday game, no natural seam anywhere in the schedule — no
gentle moment to hand over a throne. Which means the succession, when it
comes, will not be planned. It will happen mid-afternoon, after something
goes wrong, in front of everyone.

Everybody in the building knows how this ends. Nobody will say so, and the
waiting is the whole season.

## The Lord

**Kirk Cousins**, temporarily, and everyone involved knows the word.

## What Changed This Spring

**Cousins**, **Linderbaum**, **Kwity Paye**, **Nakobe Dean** — and
**Geno Smith** sent to the Jets. A house buying competence in bulk after
three wins, while the first pick in the realm waits his turn.

## Prophecy

That Mendoza plays before Christmas, and that everyone pretends this was the plan.
