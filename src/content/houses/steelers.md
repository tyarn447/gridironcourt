## The Wound

Six titles — more than anyone. And the last one was in February 2009.

This house does not collapse. That is precisely the problem. It goes 10–7,
it goes 9–8, it makes the postseason often enough to keep hope structurally
sound, and it has not won a playoff game since January 2017. Pittsburgh has
turned mediocrity into a nine-year institution and calls it stability.

The wound isn't losing. It's the flatness.

## The Lord

**Aaron Rodgers**, at forty-two, on twenty-two million guaranteed.

A four-time MVP arriving at the end of a long and complicated career, in the
one house in the realm whose entire identity is *seriousness*. It is either
the last great signing of an era or a house so out of ideas it bought a name.
Nobody in Pittsburgh has decided which, including the people who did it.

## The Knights

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Ravens.** The realm's most violent standing appointment.

## What Changed This Spring

**Rodgers.** **Pittman.** A hundred million for **Nick Herbig**. This house
looked at nine years of nearly and decided the answer was to get older and
more expensive immediately.

## Prophecy

One playoff win. It has been so long that this now counts as ambition.
