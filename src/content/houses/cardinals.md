## The Wound

This is the oldest continuously operating house in the sport. It began in
Chicago in 1898. It has one championship, from 1947, won in a city it left
in 1960 for another city it left in 1988.

A hundred and twenty-eight years. One title. It is the longest sustained
disappointment in American sport and the realm barely registers it, which
is somehow the worst part.

## The Lord

**Jacoby Brissett**, who is thirty-three and has spent his career being the
competent man a house turns to when the plan has failed.

Because the plan failed. This house **released Kyler Murray** — the first
overall pick it built a decade around — and he signed in Minnesota the next
day. Arizona is starting over with a career backup and the worst odds in the
realm.

## The Knights

- **Tyler Allgeier** — arrived this spring. That is the extent of the news.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Rams, House 49ers, House Seahawks.** Arizona is the fourth house
  in the strongest division in the sport and has been for some time.

## What Changed This Spring

They **released Kyler Murray** and signed **Brissett** and **Allgeier**.
That first move is the whole offseason and most of the decade: a house
admitting that ten years of building around one man produced nothing, and
starting again with no one in particular.

## Prophecy

None worth writing. The realm ranks them thirty-second of thirty-two.
