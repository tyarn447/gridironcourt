## The Wound

This house's problem is that it already had the perfect story. Twice.

In February 2008 and again in February 2012 it walked into the final field
as the lesser house and beat New England — the first time ending a perfect
season, in what is still the most famous upset the sport has produced.

Nothing since has come close, and nothing ever will. New York has spent
fifteen years as a bad team with two of the best memories in the realm,
which is its own kind of trap.

## The Lord

**Jaxson Dart**, in his second season, on a house that won four games. He is
young and unproven and the entire estate is on his shoulders because there
is no one else.

## The Knights

- **Odell Beckham Jr.** — returned. Older, slower, and carrying a catch this
  city has not stopped talking about since 2014. This is sentiment more than
  strategy and no one is pretending otherwise.
- **Isaiah Likely** — forty million, and the actual plan.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Eagles.** The most venomous rivalry in the division, and the one
  where the crowds genuinely dislike each other.
- **House Cowboys.** Ancient, national, and mostly conducted through
  television.
- **House Patriots.** Not a rivalry. A haunting, and New York is the ghost.

## What Changed This Spring

**Beckham** and **JuJu Smith-Schuster** returned on nostalgia, **Likely** and
**Tremaine Edmunds** arrived on purpose, and **Dexter Lawrence** — the best
player in the building — was traded to Cincinnati. That last one is the move
that tells you what this house actually thinks of its chances.

## Prophecy

That the young lord shows them something before the year is out.
