## The Wound

Two things happened to this house that it has never resolved.

It left Baltimore in the middle of the night in 1984 — moving vans, snow, a
city waking up to nothing. And it spent the 2000s with one of the greatest
lords the sport has produced and converted all of it into a single title,
which the realm still considers a waste.

Since then: a decade of searching. This house has started a new quarterback
almost every year since 2019 and has treated each one as the answer.

## The Lord

**Daniel Jones**, eighty-eight million dollars, sixty guaranteed.

Written off in New York, he arrived here and was, briefly and genuinely,
excellent — before an Achilles tore and ended it. This house paid him
anyway. It is the most sincere bet in the realm: that the good version was
real and the injury was just an injury.

## The Knights

- **Alec Pierce** — a hundred and fourteen million to stay, after this house
  sent Michael Pittman to Pittsburgh. A clear statement about who they think
  is better.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Patriots.** The defining rivalry of the last era, and Indianapolis
  lost most of it.
- **House Texans, House Titans, House Jaguars.** The division, such as it is.

## What Changed This Spring

They paid **Jones**, paid **Pierce**, and let **Pittman** go. Everything this
house did was a bet that last year's brief, interrupted good stretch was the
truth.

## Prophecy

That the Achilles was the only thing wrong.
