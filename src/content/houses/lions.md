## The Wound

This house has never played in a Super Bowl. It is one of a very small
number that can say so, and it has been failing for longer than almost
anyone — a single playoff win between 1958 and 2024, an entire winless
season in 2008.

Then, recently, it became genuinely excellent, and the realm fell in love
with it, and it still has not reached the final field. Detroit's wound used
to be humiliation. Now it is something sharper: getting close enough to
believe.

Last year: 9–8, and out.

## The Lord

**Jared Goff.** Discarded by Los Angeles in a trade about a more famous man,
and rebuilt here into something better than he ever was there. He is proof
that this house's whole theory — that unwanted people can be made good — is
sound.

## The Knights

- **Jack Campbell** — on the Watch, extended at eighty-one million, fifty-one
  and a half guaranteed.
- **Isiah Pacheco** — arrived from Kansas City for almost nothing, after this
  house sent David Montgomery to Houston.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Packers, House Bears, House Vikings.** Detroit spent decades as the
  division's designated victim and has only recently stopped being that.

## What Changed This Spring

**Campbell** extended, **Pacheco** in, **Montgomery** out. Quiet work. The
realm has Detroit as the best bounce-back candidate in the sport and gives
them the easiest schedule of anyone.

## Prophecy

The final field. They have never been. It is the only thing left.
