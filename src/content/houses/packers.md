## The Wound

Thirteen championships, more than any house in the realm. A stadium that is
a place of pilgrimage. And the bannermen literally own the team — no lord
above them, no owner to hate. It is the most beloved arrangement in the sport.

The wound is what they have done with two of the greatest quarterbacks who
ever lived. Twenty-nine consecutive seasons with a Hall of Fame lord under
centre, and **one** throne to show for it, in February 2011. The realm
considers this among the great wastes in the history of the sport, and Green
Bay is quietly aware.

## The Lord

**Jordan Love.** The third in the sequence, still establishing whether he
belongs in it. Last year: 9–7–1, and a first-round exit at Chicago, 31–27,
which is the worst possible way for this house to end a season.

## The Knights

- **Devonte Wyatt** — extended at fifty-seven million.
- **Javon Hargrave** — twenty-three million, added to the Watch.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Bears.** The oldest rivalry in the sport. Green Bay is winning it
  and has been for thirty years.
- **House Vikings, House Lions.** Cold, close, and unpleasant.

## What Changed This Spring

They lost **Rashan Gary** to Dallas and **Dontayvion Wicks** to Philadelphia,
and reinvested in the Watch with **Wyatt** and **Hargrave**. A lateral
offseason for a house that lost in the first round.

## Prophecy

That the third lord is the one who doesn't waste it.
