## The Wound

Thirteen wins. Thirteen, in a house that has spent most of its existence
being a punchline about attendance and heat — and then a home loss in the
first round of the postseason to Buffalo, 27–24, and the whole thing was
over in a single afternoon.

That is the Jacksonville wound in miniature. This house has had exactly two
genuinely good eras and both ended the moment the games started counting
double.

## The Lord

**Trevor Lawrence.** The most anticipated prospect of his generation, now
deep enough into his career that "potential" has stopped being a kind word.
Thirteen wins says the answer is yes. One January afternoon says wait.

## The Knights

- **Travon Walker** — on the Watch, a hundred and ten million to stay.
- **Brenton Strange** — extended at forty-eight million, quietly.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Titans.** The nearest thing this house has to a genuine enemy, and
  it took decades to develop.
- Mostly, Jacksonville's grudge is against the realm's assumption that it
  shouldn't exist.

## What Changed This Spring

Retention, not acquisition — **Walker**, **Strange**, **Montaric Brown** all
kept. This house went 13–4 and decided the roster was already right. That is
either confidence or a failure to read the room.

## Prophecy

That thirteen wins was the floor and not the ceiling.
