## The Wound

For two decades this house was a joke, and not a gentle one — an owner the
whole realm despised, a name it took thirty years of pressure to change,
seasons that existed only to be endured.

Then a young lord arrived and the place went briefly, gloriously mad, and it
turned out the bannermen had never stopped waiting. That is the wound now:
they know how good it feels, they got a taste, and last year they went 5–12
and it was gone again.

## The Lord

**Jayden Daniels.** Electric, beloved, and the first genuinely thrilling
thing to happen to this city since the 1990s. Last season went badly around
him. This season is about whether the surroundings can be fixed before the
excitement wears off.

## The Knights

- **Odafe Oweh** — on the Watch, a hundred million dollars with sixty-eight
  guaranteed, which is an enormous statement for a house at five wins.
- **Laremy Tunsil** — the Shieldwall, extended at sixty million, to keep the
  young lord alive.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Cowboys.** The oldest, nastiest rivalry this house has, and for
  most of this century a one-way street.
- **House Eagles, House Giants.** Divisional and long-running.

## What Changed This Spring

**Oweh** for a hundred million, **Tunsil** extended, **Chigoziem Okonkwo**
added. This house spent like a contender after a twelve-loss season. Every
dollar of it was about protecting or supporting one man.

## Prophecy

That the lord stays upright, and that last year was the surroundings and not him.
