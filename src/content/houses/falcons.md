## The Wound

Twenty-eight to three.

That is not a score to this house. It is a wound with a number, and the
number is said aloud by opposing bannermen in bars, at weddings, for the
rest of time. In February 2017 Atlanta led the final game of the year by
twenty-five points in the third quarter and lost it. Nobody has ever done
that before or since.

Everything this house has done in the decade since has been read through
that afternoon.

## The Lord

**Tua Tagovailoa**, signed for the league minimum.

Read that again. A former top-five pick, a man who has led the sport in
passing, arrived here on the smallest contract available — because Miami was
done and nobody else called. It is the most quietly brutal transaction of
the offseason, and if it works it is the best story in the realm.

## The Knights

- **Drake London** — a hundred and forty-one million to stay. The one thing
  this house was never going to lose.
- **Jahan Dotson** — fifteen million, added.

## Ancient Grudges

- **House Saints.** Genuine, regional, and nastier than the rest of the
  division combined.
- **House Patriots.** Not a rivalry. The scene of the crime.

## What Changed This Spring

**Tua** on the minimum, **London** extended enormously, **Dotson** added.
A house at the bottom of the realm's expectations that spent its money on
one man and got its quarterback for free.

## Prophecy

Modest. But the lord costs nothing, so the downside is only feelings.
