> *We Are Twelve.*
> — the words of House Seahawks

Nine months ago, House Patriots stood on the last field of the winter and lost
the throne to House Seahawks.

Tonight, on the first night of the new year, the realm did the cruelest thing
it knows how to do.

It made them do it again.

---

**They lost their lord in the first half.** Seattle's — the golden one, the one
the whole rainy north has organized itself around. He went down and he did not
get up on his own, and the Drowned Hall, the loudest hall in the realm, went so
quiet you could hear the weather.

And out of the dark at the edge of the field came a man nobody in that building
was hoping for.

[BACKUP] has been in this league [YEARS] years. He has been a placeholder in six
houses. He has held a clipboard through two winters at a time and been told,
gently, every spring, that he was valued. He is not the golden one. He was never
going to be the golden one.

He is thirty-something years old and he has been waiting his entire life for a
room to be this quiet.

Reader. He won the game.

---

**Meanwhile, on the other sideline,** House Patriots was having the worst kind
of good night.

They bought a man this spring. The best receiver they have ever paid for —
brought in specifically to give their young lord someone to throw to, someone to
grow old with, the whole arrangement blessed and announced and celebrated. First
night in the colors.

He came off the field early and he did not come back.

You cannot script this. You would not be allowed to.

---

**And then there is Drake Maye,** who I am going to call by his name because
tonight he did not get to be a legend, he only got to be a young man.

This was supposed to be the answer. New year, new weapons, the ghost of last
winter exorcised on the first Sunday. Instead he watched a career backup — a man
with none of his gifts and all of his patience — take it from him, in the same
building, against the same house, for the second time in nine months.

He is very good. He is going to be great. Neither of those things helps tonight.

**[SCORE].**

---

**What it means:** Seattle may have lost their lord for a while and won the night
anyway, which is the sort of thing houses remember in January. New England got the
rematch they wanted, at the worst possible time, and paid for it with the one man
they bought to make sure this never happened again.

in that room."
*Villain of the week:* the schedule-maker, and he knows exactly what he did.
